# the loop, named

[[soft3]] drew its own picture: 7 = 4 + 2 + 1. four motions, two guarantees, one attractor. every verb is a component's own verb from [[status]]. see it at [soft3.org/scheme](https://soft3.org/scheme)

the motions, live:

| motion | what happens |
|---|---|
| [[link]] | a thought becomes [[cyberlinks]] |
| [[transmit]] | cells share one graph |
| [[render]] | the [[focus]] becomes world |
| [[measure]] | the world becomes the next link |

the guarantees sit on the channel: [[encrypt]] (every link is owned) and [[prove]] (every claim is checked). the centre is a noun — [[focus]], φ* — with converge · agree · think as its verbs
