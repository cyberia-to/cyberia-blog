# cyb 0.4 · 0.5 · 0.6

three cuts of the immortal [[cyb|robot]] in two days. get it at [cyb.ai](https://cyb.ai) or:

```bash
cargo install cyb
cy
```

## 0.4 — four bodies

ubuntu and windows join mac and android. one robot, four surfaces, built on the [[cybernode]]. the node builds are wired; a release is something you can actually run

## 0.5 — twenty-one parts

[[anatomy]] is the source of truth: 21 parts, six layers. [[ward]] is the twenty-first — the enforcer of the soul, the identity layer. [[state]] is how the robot reads external truth. the graph of the robot itself took MECE shape: `root/` dissolved into six shelves. every doc, world and crate names things by the anatomy

## 0.6 — press prove

proving is visible on-chain in seconds. departure casts on prove-press; the meter arms at start; checkpoint countdown lives on the card. you press prove, you watch the chain answer

the robot belongs to its owner, runs on any surface, and answers to no one else
